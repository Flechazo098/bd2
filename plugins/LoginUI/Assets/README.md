# Discord brand assets

`Discord-Symbol.png` and `Discord-Wordmark.png` are derived from the official
Discord Brand Guidelines assets, retrieved on 2026-09-29:

- <https://discord.com/branding>
- <https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6257adef93867e50d84d30e2/66e278299a53f5bf88615e90_Symbol.svg>
- <https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6257adef93867e50d84d30e2/67ac9b4644222140ae614b06_Wordmark.svg>

The SVG files were rendered with a transparent background by a headless local
browser and normalized to RGBA PNG with FFmpeg. Discord's marks remain subject
to Discord's brand guidelines and are not covered by this repository's
license.
